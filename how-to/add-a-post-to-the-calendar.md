# Add a post to the calendar

1. Open [the calendar](../calendar/) and the month you want.
2. Click **Edit this page**.
3. Copy this line, paste it into the **Posts** table in date order, and fill it in:

```
| Tue 6 Oct | Facebook · Instagram | Pillar | Purpose | What, in one line | Material from | — | idea |
```

4. Commit changes.

Before you add it, answer four questions — if you cannot, it is not ready to be planned:

- **Audience** — who is it for?
- **Objective** — which of our purposes does it serve? (Connect · Events · Volunteers · Funding · Families)
- **Message** — what is the one thing they should remember?
- **Action** — what should they do next?

Then ask the person closest to the activity for the material: **3–5 photos, three facts, one sentence of context.**

## A new month

Copy the previous month's page, name it `YYYY-MM.md`, empty the tables, add a link in [how the calendar works](../calendar/README.md). Plan two months ahead: in the last week of each month, fill the month after next.

Column meanings: [how the calendar works](../calendar/README.md).
