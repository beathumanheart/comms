# Publish a post

## Before

- [ ] It is on the [calendar](../calendar/) — or the owner said yes ([sync rule](../guidelines.md#3-sync-first--the-one-rule)).
- [ ] Facts checked: date, time, place, names, links.
- [ ] **Consent**: no identifiable child without the family's written consent. In doubt → hands, backs, landscapes.
- [ ] People named in the post know they are named.
- [ ] Funded project (`CES` / `ENR` in the calendar)? Logo on the image and funding sentence in the caption — wording in [funded projects](../funded-projects/).
- [ ] Languages: as the channel requires (see [the channel list](../channels.md)).

## Publish

Post from the channel's own account. Tag partners who are mentioned.

## After

- [ ] Calendar: set status to `published` and paste the link.
- [ ] Funded project? Add a line to the [evidence log](../funded-projects/evidence-log.md) **today**.
- [ ] Photos used: move them to the *published* folder in Drive.
