## What we must do

Grant agreement, **Article 17.4 — Communication, publicity and visibility**: the beneficiary must acknowledge the support received under the European Solidarity Corps in **all promotional and communication materials, including websites and social media**.

The last report received a remark on this point. For the next report we need evidence for the volunteers it covers (Martina, Marie, Chiara).

## What "acknowledge" means in practice

On every post, page, poster or flyer about the volunteers or their activities:

1. **The EU emblem** with the funding statement next to it, written in full:
   - EN: *Funded by the European Union* or *Co-funded by the European Union*
   - PT: *Financiado pela União Europeia* or *Cofinanciado pela União Europeia*

   Which of the two applies: **check the agreement.**
2. **Do not alter the emblem** — no recolouring, no cropping, no merging with other graphics.
3. **Size** — if other logos appear, the EU emblem is at least as big as the biggest of them.
4. **Name the programme** in the text: European Solidarity Corps / Corpo Europeu de Solidariedade.

Official files (emblem + statement, all languages): https://ec.europa.eu/regional_policy/information-sources/logo-download-center_en
Rules: https://commission.europa.eu/funding-tenders/managing-your-project/communicating-and-raising-eu-visibility_pt

### On social media

- Emblem + statement on the image (or as the last slide of a carousel / last frame of a video).
- One sentence in the caption, for example:
  *"[Name] is volunteering with us through the European Solidarity Corps, funded by the European Union."*

### On the website

- Emblem + statement on every page that describes the volunteering project (today: the Partnerships page — it has none in the text).
