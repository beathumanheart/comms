## How to do it

**The funding sentence** (as used on the public page):

> Operação cofinanciada pela União Europeia através do Programa Alentejo 2030 (FSE+).

**The logos** — always use the official co-financing bar (the three logos together, as at the bottom of the public page); keep the original file in the shared folder. Do not rebuild it from separate logos, recolour it or crop it.

Where it goes:

- **Social media posts** about activities of the operation: bar on the image or last slide + the funding sentence in the caption.
- **Website**: bar + sentence on pages describing these activities.
- **Events**: bar on posters, flyers and sign-up pages.
- **Publications**: bar on the cover or back page.

## Evidence

Reimbursement requests need proof. Every publication goes into the [evidence log](evidence-log.md) on the day it is published: link + screenshot.
