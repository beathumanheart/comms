# External comms guidelines

One page. If a rule is not here, use common sense and ask the owner ([who is that this season?](seasons/2026-27/owners.md)).

## 1. Why we communicate

In order of priority:

1. **Connect** with similar projects.
2. **Promote events.**
3. **Find extra volunteers and extra funding.**
4. *Maybe:* attract new families.

Every post or message should serve one of these. If it serves none, it probably does not need to go out.

## 2. Who speaks for whom

| Voice | Speaks about | Owner role |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning Community** | Education, families, daily life at Quinta das Avelãs and Beirã Station | Learning Community comms |
| **Descalças** (cooperative) | Terrain, funding, partnerships, funded projects, institutional matters | Descalças comms |
| **CES volunteers** | Their own volunteering experience | The volunteers, coordinated by Descalças |
| **Planeta Alecrim** (association) | Its own channels and content — a separate legal entity | Planeta Alecrim |

The educational project is shared; the legal entities stay distinct. Do not speak for an entity you do not own.

## 3. Sync first — the one rule

**Check with the owner *before* it goes out** when the communication is on behalf of Descalças or the Learning Community and is any of these:

- **Institutional** — to the municipality, funders, authorities, partner organisations, press or media.
- **About money** — fundraising, crowdfunding, donation appeals, paid ads, prices.
- **A call** — for volunteers, for new families, for enrolment.
- **A funded project** — anything mentioning CES or Enraizar no Interior (logos and wording are mandatory: see [funded projects](funded-projects/)).
- **About our status** — any statement on the legal or educational status of the project.
- **A new channel or a website change** — opening an account, changing pages, changing logos or names.
- **A partnership** — announcing or naming a partner.

**No sync needed:**

- Posts already on the agreed [calendar](calendar/).
- Sharing or reposting something we already published.
- Small, informal, local initiatives that stay outside the main channels.

Sync means a short message to the owner and a yes back. Silence is not a yes. Aim to answer within two working days.

## 4. Before anything is published

- [ ] It serves one of the purposes in section 1.
- [ ] Facts are right: date, time, place, names, links.
- [ ] **Consent**: no identifiable child without the family's written consent. When in doubt — hands, backs, landscapes.
- [ ] Funded project? Logo and funding sentence are there.
- [ ] After publishing: tick it in the calendar; if funded, add it to the [evidence log](funded-projects/evidence-log.md).

## 5. The route to publish

**Activity → Capture → Shape · Check · Approve → Publish + Archive**

Whoever is closest to an activity supplies the material (photos, three facts, one sentence of context) — the *proximity principle*. The comms team shapes and publishes. Details: [how it fits together](architecture/).
